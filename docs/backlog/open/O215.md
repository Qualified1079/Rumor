# O215

**`[TODO/SIM]`** Quorum for additive room authority, single-mod for subtractive (filed 2026-10-11, user idea refined)

**Does NOT fix O213** — that is stale-state (an old unexpired invite applied after a kick);
quorum changes *who authorizes*, not *when the authorization was valid*, and two mods could both have signed the old invite.
Recorded so the two are not conflated. **What it does fix: a single rogue or compromised moderator** — O50's N-of-M lesson applied to room authority.
**The asymmetry worth building:** require quorum for **additive** changes (an invite adds a reader, expands the trust surface, is hard to undo) and allow **single-mod** for **subtractive** ones (kick/ban is urgent and reversible via UNBAN).
Generalized principle:
*consensus requirement scales with how hard the action is to undo.* **Why it needs simming before building:** in a partitioned mesh a quorum decision cannot take effect until BOTH signatures reach a node, which under O55 could be days — tolerable for an invite, unacceptable for a ban.
Sim question, sharp:
how long does a quorum invite take to take effect across a partitioned mesh, and what fraction of bans would have been delayed past usefulness had they needed two signatures?
