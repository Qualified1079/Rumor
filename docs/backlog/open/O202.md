**Framing clarification (2026-08-01):
delivery does NOT gate on online/offline status — store-and-forward already holds a DM at relays and delivers on the recipient's return (proven on real wire, `LanMeshHarnessTest`).
So the risk is NOT "we over-rely on presence indicators";
it is (1) does a copy SURVIVE (TTL/eviction) and (2) is a surviving copy stored somewhere the recipient will MEET.
The creative lever is custody-based storage — preferentially keep R's DMs on nodes in R's social/breadcrumb graph (nodes that actually meet R) rather than uniform epidemic — plus presence-triggered flush (use O30 beacons as a delivery TRIGGER, not a gate) and priority persistence for undelivered DMs.** Cross-ref O55, O33, O98, O29/O58 (custody routing), O30 (presence-flush), O193 (retry/ACK is the sibling), O104.
