# O194

**`[DECISION]`** Eclipse defense = trust-weighted view, NOT radio fingerprinting (commodity RF is unreachable) (filed 2026-07-30, privacy workshop)

Q:
can a node detect it's eclipsed (surrounded by sybils feeding its entire view — every relay, every O58-Tier-1 delivery signal attacker-controlled) by telling two identities are one physical transmitter?
**Honest no, in both directions.** RF fingerprinting (carrier-frequency offset, I/Q imbalance, power-on transients) is real but needs SDR/raw-IQ PHY access;
a commodity Android Wi-Fi/BLE chip does all RF/PHY work in hardware+firmware and hands the app layer only decoded frames + coarse RSSI — no raw IQ, no per-frame carrier offset, on transmit OR receive path.
So a rotating-fresh-identity sybil in a crowded plaza is unbeatable AT THE IDENTITY LAYER (clock-skew / RSSI-geometry are slow, noisy, defeated by faked timing;
proof-of-work craters the O55 battery envelope; no TEE attestation per O56).
**Therefore the defense is not detection but refusing to let quantity mean anything:** construct the relay/round-robin view weighted by human-confirmed trust (O136 friending), NOT node count — a swarm of 100 fresh strangers gets low AGGREGATE weight vs 2 in-person friends;
mix new proximate nodes in at a capped ratio keyed PER TRUST-TIER (per-identity caps are defeated by rotation). Plus physical movement (user).
This IS O135's "make sybils harmless, not impossible." Cross-ref O135, O136, O58, O56, O55.
