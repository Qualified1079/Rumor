**`[TODO/CODE]`** Bridge-asserted-pubkey constraint on synthetic userIds**

When a Meshtastic / MeshCore radio reports a new contact, the synthetic userId must be derived as `<bridgePrefix>:<hash-of-radio-asserted-pubkey>` (not as `<bridgePrefix>:<hash-of-name>` and not as a bridge-chosen string).
A compromised or malicious radio can then substitute keys only for *new* contacts;
existing pinned contacts whose userId is derived from a previously-seen pubkey surface a verification event when the underlying pubkey appears to change (O21 display-name pinning, generalized to pubkey pinning under the synthetic prefix).
MeshCore current implementation uses `meshcore:<FNV-1a of name>` — that's spoofable; migrate to pubkey-hash before any DM bridging ships.
