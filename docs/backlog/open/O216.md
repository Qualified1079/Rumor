**`[TODO/CODE]`** There is NO way to join a room — single-use invite link + creator chain (implements O214's membership gate)

**The load-bearing hole in Rooms.** Verified 2026-10-11: invites are *moderator-pushed* — a mod names a `invitedUserId` and receivers call `addMember`.
There is **no redemption path, no token, and nothing records who invited whom** (`grep` for a join/redeem function in `:core`/`:app` returns nothing).
Consequence:
**`RoomMembershipPolicy` (OPEN/INVITE/PASSWORD/CLOSED) is persisted and never consulted by any code** — posting is enforced (O89), *joining* is not, because joining does not exist.
So membership is honor-system bookkeeping a mod maintains, not a gate.
**What O214 decided to build:** a single-use invite **link** plus a documented **creator chain**. Sketch, not yet a decision:
a mod signs an invite carrying `inviteId = H(secret)` and the secret travels out-of-band (QR/text);
the redeemer gossips a self-signed join proving knowledge of `secret`, bound to their own userId;
receivers check `H(secret) == inviteId`, the mod's signature, that the issuer is a moderator, non-expiry, and **that `inviteId` has not already been redeemed**;
on success the member record stores `invitedBy`, forming a tree traceable to the creator.
That tree IS O151's signed invite tree and is what makes subtree-prune bans possible.
**Two problems to solve honestly, not paper over:** (a) **single-use across a partition** — "first redemption wins" diverges when two nodes see different first-redeemers, which is O149's causality problem again;
needs a deterministic convergent tiebreak (lowest redeemer userId, or HLC order), not wall-clock arrival.
(b) **the secret is a bearer token gossiped in clear** — once revealed it is spent, and the legitimate redeemer normally wins because they are the source, but a malicious *first-hop relay* could suppress the real join and substitute its own.
That is inherent to bearer tokens; bound it and document it rather than claiming it away. Cross-refs O214, O151, O149, O187, O79.
