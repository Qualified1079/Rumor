# O196

**`[DECISION]`** Reject handheld cover traffic + adaptive/incremental fill — joules-not-bits; cover traffic is infrastructure-only future work (filed 2026-07-30, privacy workshop)

Two rejections recorded so the conversation doesn't recur.
(1) **"Fill spare bandwidth with dummies" on handheld devices — NO.** Idle bandwidth is not idle energy;
radio TX is among the most expensive phone operations, and the O55 device is JOULE-bound (weeks on handcrank/solar, <2%/hr), not bit-bound.
Cover traffic spends the scarcest resource to buy a property O51's local-sybil observer defeats anyway. (2) **Adaptive / incremental fill (e.g.
60→70→80%) — NO, it's a trap.** The adaptation signal correlates with the traffic it hides — if fill responds to real activity it leaks that activity;
if it responds to nothing it's just constant-rate cover traffic on a timer paying full price.
Only CONSTANT-RATE cover traffic provably hides timing, and it's maximally expensive (the website-fingerprinting literature keeps re-confirming adaptive padding breaks, constant-rate holds).
**Where cover traffic DOES belong (deferred future work, not rejected):** mains-powered fixed relays (O54/O104 window relay, USB-booted laptop) have no joule constraint and fit O58-Tier-3 Free-mode anchors — cover traffic is an INFRASTRUCTURE-NODE feature, never a handheld one, and waits until that anchor tier exists.
