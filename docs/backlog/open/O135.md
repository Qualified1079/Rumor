**`[TODO/CODE+UI]`** Sybil/peer-spam resistance — make sybils *harmless*, not impossible (design, filed 2026-07-22 from user threat-model discussion)

**Framing (extends O27/O51/O60 floor):** Ed25519 identities are free and unlimited by design;
anything keyed on identity (per-id rate limit, blocking, reputation) is defeated by cycling identities.
The goal is NOT to prevent sybils but to bound their damage.
**Four load-bearing mechanisms:** (1) **Partition every bounded resource by trust tier — and the tier is EXPLICIT USER ACTION, not "handshaken."** User correction 2026-07-22:
"known" as in *completed-an-exchange* is worthless here — every sybil is "known" the instant it handshakes.
The privileged tier must be **friended / named / verified** (an affirmative user act), NOT seen-on-the-wire. Tiers:
**friended (named/verified/DMed) > seen-but-unnamed > never-seen.** Friended peers get *guaranteed* buffer/connection/storage slots;
everyone else shares a *separate, bounded, LRU-evicted* pool. A sybil flood saturates the unnamed pool but **cannot starve friended-peer traffic**. **GAP:
there is no explicit "friend/accept" action today** — contacts auto-persist on exchange (O134), and `isVerified`/`alwaysSave`/`displayName` exist but nothing sets them via a deliberate user gesture.
A real friending mechanism (accept/friend button, on contact + on first DM) is the prerequisite for this whole tier system — build it with O110/O134.
(2) **Identity-INDEPENDENT aggregate caps** — bound the *total* regardless of identity count. Concretely (user's own analogy):
the O134 "unnamed peers" collapsible IS the bounded pool — a fixed-size bucket that sybils fill and LRU-evict each other out of, never growing unbounded and never touching the named area.
Same shape everywhere:
global solicited-pulse token bucket (O127), global inbound-session concurrency cap, storage quota+eviction (O23), bloom/RBSR cumulative caps (O117).
Sybils churn the LRU; they don't overflow it.
(3) **"Known peers only" inbox toggle** — the seam exists (`core/policy/InboxFilter.kt` — `PermissiveInboxFilter` today;
add a `KnownSendersInboxFilter` gated on the contact list).
Relay still carries everything (relay-never-filters invariant), but the *inbox* only surfaces known keys → sybil floods become invisible to the user even though the mesh relays them.
Pairs with Rooms (explicit membership) for the spam-free experience.
This is the direct answer to "blocking is useless if identities cycle" — you don't block bad keys, you allowlist good ones.
(4) **Trust acquisition over distance** (QR only works in person):
**transitive vouching/introductions with a degrees-of-separation slider** — a mutual contact signs "this key is Bob";
you accept vouches up to N hops (user-set slider).
**Why this self-defends (user's attack-tree walk, 2026-07-22, worth preserving):** a sybil churning a dozen fake peers/sec → nobody friends those → they stay in the unnamed collapsible, never flood.
Sybils friending *each other* → useless, the chain still needs a real person at the root.
So the only way in is a real person vouching for the fakes → and because **every vouch is signed and attributable, you see exactly who signed off and block that person as a valid signer in YOUR chain** (block-the-signer, not the endless fakes).
Residual attack:
"spend 20 min in an easy-access room, get a real user to friend you, then spam them a million fake peers" — but the fakes land in the friended user's *unnamed collapsible* (never floods their feed), the signer is attributable, and one block of that signer severs the whole subtree.
**May largely solve itself** — the collapsible + attributable-signed-vouch-chain + block-the-signer composition is self-limiting by construction.

A small proof-of-work admission cost for first-contact-from-unknown was *considered and is disfavored* — the O55 low-power-survival envelope makes per-message PoW too expensive on legit solar/handcrank devices, and it's asymmetric against a resourced attacker.
**Literature grounding (this is a solved-ish problem;
the user's design converges on known results):** **Douceur, "The Sybil Attack" (2002)** — the impossibility result:
without a central authority or a scarce *verifiable* resource you cannot *prevent* sybils, which is exactly why the posture is "make them harmless" (honest-docs O27).
**SybilGuard / SybilLimit (Yu et al.
2006/08)** — social-graph admission control bounds accepted sybils to ~O(log n) **per attack edge** (genuine trust link the attacker forms with an honest user), NOT per minted identity — the formal backing for "the fakes need a real person to vouch, and you block that person / sever the edge." Caveat:
relies on the honest graph being *fast-mixing*; Rumor's sparse geographically-clustered mesh may not be, so treat the bound as directional not guaranteed.
**Advogato max-flow trust metric (Levien) + PGP Web of Trust** — the vouch/degrees-of-separation mechanism;
trust flows from your seed through signed edges, capacity-limited, so the certified set is bounded by the min-cut = the attack edges;
blocking a signer removes its edge.
**Secure Scuttlebutt (SSB)** — a SHIPPING P2P gossip social mesh whose **"hops" replication setting IS the user's degrees-of-separation slider** (you replicate friends + friends-of-friends up to N hops);
the closest deployed prior art — study its follow-graph/hops model before building O135(4).
**Freenet WoT plugin** — spam resistance via trust-distance in a censorship-resistant P2P net, same shape.
**DTN buffer-management literature** (drop-oldest/drop-largest/trust-based forwarding under congestion) informs the O132/resource-partition side. **Actionable:
SSB "hops" is the reference design for the vouch/FOLLOW slider;
Advogato max-flow is the formal model for "block-the-signer severs the subtree."** **DECISION — DO NOT port SSB's BLOCK model (user 2026-07-22, overrides the research's "block-dominates-follow" port recommendation):** SSB auto-inherits the blocks of everyone you follow (forced transitive block).
Rumor keeps its **existing subscribable-blocklist model** (O67/O78 machinery:
signed `BlocklistPublish`, opt-in subscribe, layerable, per-list) — it is *strictly more controllable*:
you get automatic inheritance IF you choose to subscribe to a list, but you are never forced to inherit a block just because you follow someone, and you can layer your own + multiple curated lists granularly.
So:
**port SSB's FOLLOW/hops (the trust frontier) but NOT its BLOCK semantics.** When computing the O135(4) vouch/hops frontier, subtree-pruning uses **your own blocks + your subscribed blocklists**, never blocks auto-inherited through the follow graph.
This keeps "block-the-signer severs the subtree" (the pruning still happens) while making *which* blocks prune a granular user choice, not a forced transitive cascade.

**2026-07-25 — feed-flood sybil vector + the pre-gate analysis (from the O154 priority-model discussion;
failure modes to be fleshed out later):** attack = thousands of self-owned identities posting to each other (or to a self-made topic) to drown shared bandwidth.
Defense holds *because caps are per-resource, not per-identity*: friend-priority protects friended traffic;
the sybil topic never gets boosted so it sinks to the floor;
the floor is a bounded shared LRU budget, so 10 vs 10,000 sybils get the SAME floor share (extra identities evict each other — minting more buys nothing).
**Can you pre-gate before it hits the device?
Mostly yes above the link layer, bounded at it:** (1) **RBSR offer-first** (O42/G24) gates *bodies* — a neighbor offers a compact summary, you pull only what you want, so you pay summary-cost not body-cost;
summary/reconciliation cost is itself capped (O117).
(2) **Honest-neighbor priority** (O43) — a well-behaved neighbor won't spend its link-to-you budget relaying floor noise over prioritized traffic, so the flood only reaches your link if the sybil is your *direct* transport neighbor.
(3) **Irreducible link-layer floor** = a direct sybil neighbor: you must receive-to-classify, but bound it with **tiered connection admission** (O135(1):
friended = guaranteed slots;
unknowns = bounded-LRU pool) + **per-connection inbound rate caps** — a ceiling independent of identity count (more sybils don't enlarge the pool).
**Real residual:** floor noise degrades *passive* peer-discovery (real new peer = needle in haystack), but discovery survives via **proximity-weighting** (nearby beacon outranks distant sybil) + **active solicitation** (O124/O30, proximity-bounded, can't be flooded from across the mesh).
