**`[TODO/CODE]`** `MeshViewTracker` freshness is judged by the sender's clock (filed 2026-11-06; verified)

`record` clamps future stamps to now but trusts past ones (`routing/MeshViewTracker.kt:65`); `isStale` is `now - beaconAtMs > freshWindowMs` (:141).
A peer whose clock runs more than the window slow (the "64-day phone" case named in `time/Hlc.kt`) is stale on arrival, never enters the view, and can never be spanned into the backbone.
The `stamp <= prev` drop (:67) is also sender-clock driven, so a MOBILE exit pulse after a backwards clock step is ignored.
**Fix:** store receive time for staleness; keep the sender stamp only for ordering, per sender.
