# G19

**O2 persistent link — field-verified (closes O2)**

Once a group forms it is held open;
the client re-runs a gossip session every 10s over a fresh TCP connect (skipping discovery/GO-negotiation/DHCP, which cost ~60s/round). Teardown:
peer no longer BLE-visible (`TransportConfig.isPeerNearby`, wired to `peerNearbySignal.value`), 3 consecutive round failures, or GO-side 35s idle watchdog (the GO has no round timer — the client drives cadence).
`connectToPeer` early-returns while `groupConnected` (issuing connect() during a live group disrupts it — observed on OnePlus).
Delivery latency measured ≤10s, sessions 60ms–2.7s. Not per-contact "priority" anymore — persistence is automatic for any nearby peer;
`isPriorityPeer` bookkeeping retained. **Known limitation:** a held group may starve discovery of a third phone — revisit rotation when 3-device testing starts.
Also hardened: connect-BUSY retries are bounded (5 × 2s) and die with the in-flight flag;
on exhaustion the framework is reset via `removeGroupThenDiscover()` — two unbounded 1s retry chains previously livelocked both phones for minutes after a reinstall killed the apps mid-link.
